This site was assembled by an AI agent, supervised by a musician. The division of labor: the agent writes the code, the musician decides what's true.

It's built from [liner-notes](https://github.com/hycccc/liner-notes), an open-source template — music player, album workshop, travel map, and an optional agent-written blog pipeline. If you're a musician who wants a site like this one, fork it: everything personal lives in a handful of JSON files, and everything else is yours to keep or delete.
